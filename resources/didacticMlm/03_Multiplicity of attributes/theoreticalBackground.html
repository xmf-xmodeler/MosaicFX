LU3 - Multiplicities of Attributes

Theoretical Background

Similar to associations, each attribute must specify a multiplicity. The multiplicity of an attribute specifies the number of values that may be or must be entered for its slots. If the maximum multiplicity of an attribute is higher than one, a sequence of values must be entered. This corresponds to known data structures like arrays or lists that contain multiple values. In UML, the default multiplicity is 1..1, shown as [1] for each attribute.
