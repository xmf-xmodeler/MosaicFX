Generalization serves as an abstraction over classes and abstraction is always purpose-driven. The purpose we want to fulfill must take potential future changes into account. In order to avoid constant changes to our model, we want our abstraction to time-invariant. All four classes may be considered as a means of transportation, but the given properties of the superclass Vehicle are ill-suited for horses (a horse cannot be electric). Multiple generalizations might be reasonable in this scenario, e.g., PublicTransportation or MeansOfTransportation. That is why you should always provide a rationale for contingent modeling decisions.
